From one productto a full routine

Growth and product strategy for a natural personal-care brand: getting shoppers to buy a regime, not a single SKU, so basket size and order value rise.

AOV

The metric it targeted

Multi-SKU

The behaviour it built

01

The problem

Shoppers came for a single hero product. Each order carried one item, so order value stayed low and customers never experienced the brand as a complete routine.

02

The insight

The purchase data already showed which products people buy together. Packaged as seasonal routines, with the cart rewarding the step up, those pairings could turn one-item orders into regimes.

03

What I did

Designed data-backed routine bundles from co-purchase behaviour and launched one live combo on Flipkart that lifted perceived value and average selling price. Then built the levers around it: cart-value incentives, routine-led creatives and a pack-size review that fed new product development.

04

The four growth levers

01 · BUNDLE

Routine bundles

Combos built from co-purchase data; one launched live on Flipkart

02 · CART

Value incentives

Threshold-based freebies matched to the routine, to deepen the basket

03 · CREATIVE

Routine-led story

From product claims to seasonal regimes, reframing the path to purchase

04 · PORTFOLIO

Pack-size gaps

Priority SKUs and missing packs, driving new product development

THE CORE IDEA

Sell the routine, not the product. Every lever (what goes in the bundle, what the cart rewards, what the creative says, which packs exist) pushes the same behaviour: buying several products that work together.
